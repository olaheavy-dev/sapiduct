What is the difference between speed and velocity?
search("speed versus velocity") · 2 results · sample

Speed says how fast something moves; velocity adds the direction.

1kinematics-chapter.pdf · p. 4"Speed has magnitude only. Velocity has magnitude and direction."

A car circling a track at a steady speed is still changing its velocity, because its direction keeps changing.

2kinematics-chapter.pdf · p. 4"…an object moving in a circle at constant speed is still changing its velocity."

Average velocity divides displacement by time; average speed divides total distance by time.

3lecture-03-transcript.vtt · 12:40"Average velocity is displacement divided by elapsed time."
