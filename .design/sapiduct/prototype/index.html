What is the difference between speed and velocity?
search("speed versus velocity") · 2 results · sample

Speed says how fast something moves; velocity adds the direction.

1kinematics-chapter.pdf · p. 4"Speed has magnitude only. Velocity has magnitude and direction."

A car circling a track at a steady speed is still changing its velocity, because its direction keeps changing.

2kinematics-chapter.pdf · p. 4"…an object moving in a circle at constant speed is still changing its velocity."

Average velocity divides displacement by time; average speed divides total distance by time.

3lecture-03-transcript.vtt · 12:40"Average velocity is displacement divided by elapsed time."

Numbers and text
Session 2 digest · built from notes-02.md and lecture-02-transcript.vtt · sample

        
Python treats 3 and "3" as different things: one is a number, the other is text.
1notes-02.md · section 1Where numbers and text are introduced as different types. (Sample.)

        
Adding two numbers adds them; adding two pieces of text joins them, so "3" + "4" gives "34".
2lecture-02-transcript.vtt · 06:30The worked example of joining text with +. (Sample.)

        
int() turns text that looks like a whole number into a number, and str() turns a value into text.
3notes-02.md · section 3The conversion functions and their limits. (Sample.)

        
Mixing a number and text in one + raises a TypeError, and the message names the line to fix.
4lecture-02-transcript.vtt · 18:05Reading the error message. (Sample.)
